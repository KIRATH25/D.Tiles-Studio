Our Philosophy

Fewer surfaces. Better chosen.

Most tile showrooms ask you to choose from thousands of options under harsh lights. We do the opposite. Every surface in our studio is selected for honest materials, accurate colour, dimensional precision and how gracefully it ages.

Then we help you see it properly — full-size slabs under warm and cool light, swatches you can take home, and specialists who will tell you when a glossy tile is the wrong choice for your bathroom floor.

Good to know
Frequently asked

      

        
How do I order from the website?
Choose Buy Now on any product, or add several products to your cart and tap “Send Cart on WhatsApp”. Your complete order — product names, SKUs, quantities and estimated totals — opens as a pre-written WhatsApp message to our studio. We confirm availability, final price with GST and delivery before you pay.

        
Do you deliver outside Ghaziabad?
Yes. We deliver across Delhi-NCR (Ghaziabad, Noida, Greater Noida, East Delhi, Gurugram) and ship Pan-India through trusted freight partners.

        
Are samples free?
Your first three swatches are complimentary. Courier dispatch is a flat ₹150, or collect them free from our Indirapuram showroom.

        
How many extra tiles should I order?
We recommend 10% extra for standard straight layouts, 15% for diagonal, and 20% for herringbone. Our calculator includes this automatically.

        
Do you provide GST invoices?
Yes — every order comes with a GST-compliant invoice. Trade customers can share their GSTIN on the quotation form.
