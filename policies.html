Studio policies

Privacy Policy

D.Tiles Studio respects your privacy. This website does not require an account and does not collect payment details.

  • Enquiries: When you tap a WhatsApp button, the details you typed (such as name, pincode and product selection) are placed into a WhatsApp message on your own device. Nothing is sent until you press send inside WhatsApp.
  • Your cart & preferences: Your cart, saved surfaces, swatch box and contact details are stored only in your own browser so forms can be pre-filled. You can clear them at any time by clearing your browser's site data.
  • Use of information: Information you send us on WhatsApp is used only to respond to your enquiry, prepare quotations and deliver orders. We never sell your data.

Terms of Service

  • Prices shown are estimates in Indian Rupees and exclude GST and delivery unless stated. Final prices are confirmed by the studio on WhatsApp or invoice.
  • Product imagery is representative. Natural stone and stone-look surfaces vary between batches; we recommend viewing samples or full slabs before ordering.
  • An order is confirmed only after the studio confirms availability and you complete payment through an agreed method.
  • Always order sufficient quantity from a single batch; later batches may differ in shade.

Shipping & Delivery

  • Delhi-NCR: Same-day or next-day delivery for in-stock items to Ghaziabad, Noida and East Delhi; 1–3 days elsewhere in NCR.
  • Pan-India: Freight delivery in 4–8 working days, quoted per order.
  • Made-to-order batches: 7–14 days from confirmation.
  • Unloading: Kerbside delivery is standard. Floor-wise carrying can be arranged at an additional charge.
  • Returns: Unopened, full boxes in resaleable condition may be returned within 7 days of delivery. Cut, laid or special-order items cannot be returned.

GST Information

All invoices are GST-compliant. Tiles and sanitaryware are generally taxed at 18% GST; the exact rate is confirmed on your invoice. Business customers can share their GSTIN on the quotation form to claim input tax credit.

Care & Maintenance

  • Clean daily with a soft mop and pH-neutral cleaner. Avoid acid or bleach on honed, polished and natural-stone-look finishes.
  • Use felt pads under furniture legs to protect polished surfaces.
  • Seal cement-based grout once a year in wet areas, or choose epoxy grout for stain-free joints.
  • Keep a few spare tiles from the same batch for future repairs.
