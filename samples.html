            Architectural Field Notes
            
Why Test Samples In Situ?

            
A studio surface never lives in isolation. It breathes alongside your orientation, ceiling heights, and cabinetry.

            
1. Daylight OrientationNorth-facing rooms receive cooler, blue-tinted illumination where cream stones can flatten. South/West light intensifies warm terracotta and travertine veining dramatically around late afternoon.

            
2. 3000K vs. 4000K LightingCommercial spotlights often distort micro-crystalline depth. Place your swatches flat under your intended cove or downlight temperature to witness the genuine slip finish and gloss reflection.

            
3. Veneer & Paint InterplayHold the tile directly beside your scheduled oak veneers, brass hardware, or wall emulsion swatches to confirm mineral tone balance without synthetic surprises.
